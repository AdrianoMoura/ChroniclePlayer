# D-049

**Spec:** `youtube-api.md`

**Ordinary per-cycle RSS sync failures no longer show a banner — only a systemic failure does (every polled channel failing at once, on a poll wide enough that random noise couldn't explain it)** (`bugs.md` [[B-110]]) — supersedes the original behavior, which showed a "{count} channel(s) failed" banner (with expandable per-channel details) any time `SyncReport.outcome === 'partial'`, i.e. any cycle with at least one failed and one succeeded channel. Once [[D-048]]'s live investigation confirmed YouTube's own RSS backend routinely returns transient 404/500 for genuinely active channels — independent of Chronicle, non-deterministic (the same channel can fail one cycle and succeed the next) — it became clear `outcome: 'partial'` would be true on essentially *every* cycle forever (never converging to zero, since it's a rolling noise floor across ~230 channels each queried independently), making the banner a permanent fixture the owner could never act on or dismiss for good — exactly the kind of "something on screen nobody put there for a reason" the calm/predictable-UI principle (`CLAUDE.md`) argues against, and worse, training the owner to stop reading sync banners at all (alarm fatigue), which would blunt a *real* failure signal too. **Rationale (the product owner's own call, 2026-07-16):** stop surfacing per-cycle partial failures entirely — they're retried automatically next cycle regardless (D-048) — and reserve the banner for a signal actually worth the owner's attention: every polled channel failing in the same cycle (`outcome === 'failed'`, gated on `channelsPolled > 1` so a single unlucky manual per-channel refresh, [[B-036]], can't trip it from ordinary noise alone) — a pattern random per-channel RSS flakiness essentially cannot produce on its own, so it's a reliable proxy for a real problem (e.g. the user's own network being down). **Exercised 2026-07-16:** `App.tsx`'s `refresh:done` handler now checks `outcome === 'failed' && channelsPolled > 1` instead of `outcome === 'partial'`; the old `app.banner.refreshPartial` string is replaced by `app.banner.refreshAllFailed`. Ordinary partial failures still get logged (`sync_log`, `SyncReport.failures`) for anyone who goes looking — they're just not pushed at the owner as a banner anymore.
