## D-053 — Live/ended broadcasts sort and bucket by effective date, not just publishedAt

**What prompted it:** the owner noticed a livestream running for hours kept sinking
below videos published since it started, sorted purely by its original (older)
`publishedAt` — the opposite of what you'd want for content that's still actionable
right now, or that just wrapped up.

**What shipped:** every video now has an `effectiveDate` that drives both its sort
position and which date bucket (Today/Yesterday/This Week/Earlier) it lands in. A
video that was never live keeps using its plain `publishedAt`, unchanged. A currently-
live video sorts to the top of *Today*, however long ago it actually started, for as
long as it's still going. A video whose broadcast has ended sorts and buckets by when
it actually ended (`liveStreamingDetails.actualEndTime`, newly captured — new
`videos.live_ended_at` column, schema v12) rather than when it started — so a stream
that ran for hours, or one that crossed midnight, now shows under the date it actually
wrapped, not buried under its original start date/position. Two earlier designs were
floated and revised in the same conversation before landing here: a separate
bucket-less "Live now" section (rejected — the owner wanted this to stay "just
ordering," not a new section), then a same-bucket-only reorder that sorted correctly
within a bucket but still grouped a midnight-crossing broadcast under the wrong day
(caught by the owner's own follow-up question).

**Also extended, same conversation:** the feed's existing gray "ended" badge
(B-114) only ever kept a livestream's badge alive via the sticky `wasLive` flag, which
requires Chronicle to have observed the video while it was genuinely live. The new
`liveEndedAt` signal doesn't have that requirement — YouTube reports
`actualEndTime` as permanent video metadata regardless of when the video is first
hydrated — so a broadcast discovered only after it already ended (e.g. via
gap-backfill) now shows the correct badge too, not just the correct ordering.

Full narrative, including both rejected/revised design iterations and the schema/API
plumbing, is in `../decisions.md` (D-053). Checked via
`npm run typecheck && npm run lint && npm test`; live-verified by the owner after a
full wipe/resync against a real channel and a real ended broadcast — the ordering
and end-time capture were both confirmed correct directly against the local database.
